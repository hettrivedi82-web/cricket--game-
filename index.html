How to play
• The bowler runs in and delivers. Hit the ball just as it reaches the golden ring.
• Choose your shot direction: tap the left, middle or right side of the screen (or the buttons). PC: A / SPACE / D.
• Look at the fielders! Hit into a gap for extra runs. Hit at a fielder and he stops it.
• Perfect timing = SIX 🔥, slightly off = FOUR, then 1–3 runs. Too early or too late = OUT!
• Boundary after boundary builds a COMBO bonus. Leave wide balls alone for +1 run.
• 12 balls, 3 wickets. Beat your own high score on each difficulty!
